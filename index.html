Bracket Brawl — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Who runs this app

Bracket Brawl is made and run by Saief Al-faour, a private developer in Denmark. I am the "data controller" — the person responsible for your data under the GDPR (the EU General Data Protection Regulation).

Contact: saief1985@proton.me

What data the app keeps, and why

The app keeps only what it needs to work. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

  • Your email address. To create your account, log you in, and send you the sign-up and password-reset emails.
  • Your password. To log you in. Our hosting provider stores only a scrambled form (a bcrypt hash), never the plain password.
  • An account ID. A random code that links your data to your account.
  • Player names you type in. Your saved player list, so you can reuse names.
  • Your tournaments. Name, bracket, match formats, scores, and when it was created, last changed and finished. So you can open your tournaments again, on any phone.
  • Login records. Our hosting provider logs each login, which can include your IP address and device type, and keeps account dates (created, last login). This keeps accounts secure.

Player names may be names of other people (for example your friends). Only type in names you are fine with storing. Use nicknames if you prefer.

On your phone: the app saves your login so you stay logged in. It is removed when you log out or delete your account.

Legal basis (why we are allowed to use it)

  • Your email, password, account ID, players and tournaments: needed to give you the service you signed up for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), "performance of a contract").
  • Login records: our legitimate interest in keeping accounts safe (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore) — hosts the login system and the database. Supabase handles the data only on my behalf. Privacy page: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Google (Gmail) — delivers the sign-up and password-reset emails. Google receives your email address and the email itself (with the login link). Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple — delivers the app through the App Store. Apple's own privacy policy applies to your Apple account and App Store use.

I never sell your data and never share it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored on Supabase servers in West EU (Ireland), inside the EU.

Data may leave the EU. Your data is stored in Ireland, but Supabase is a Singapore company and Google is a US company, so they may access it from outside the EU. Supabase is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (EU-approved contract terms) in its data processing agreement. Google uses the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long it is kept

  • Your account, players and tournaments: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, your login, players and tournaments are deleted at once from the live database.
  • Login records held by Supabase are deleted after 1 hour.
  • There are no backups, so deleted data cannot come back.
  • Account emails stay in the Gmail sender's "Sent" folder until deleted there [unverified — depends on Gmail settings].

How to delete your account

In the app: Home → Settings → Delete my account, then confirm. This deletes your login and all your players and tournaments. It cannot be undone.

You can also delete single players or tournaments in the app at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • ask to see the data I hold about you (access),
  • ask me to correct it,
  • ask me to delete it,
  • ask me to limit (restrict) how I use it,
  • ask for a copy of it in a common format (data portability),
  • object to the use of your login records.

To use any of these, email saief1985@proton.me. I will reply within one month. For hard requests this can be extended by two more months, and I will tell you if so.

You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet): https://www.datatilsynet.dk/english/file-a-complaint — Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark — dt@datatilsynet.dk.

Children

You must be at least 15 years old to create an account. (15 is the Danish age limit in the Danish Data Protection Act, § 6(2).)
